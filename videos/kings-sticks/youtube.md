## Title
The King's Trick That Made a Thief Catch Himself 👑 #Shorts

## Description
A necklace vanishes from a locked throne room, and the King never even has to search for it.

Twelve guards, one thief. The King hands every guard the same wooden stick and says the thief's stick will grow one inch by morning. One guard grins all night. In the morning eleven sticks match and one is shorter. The sticks were plain wood from the woodshed and could never grow, so only the guilty guard had a reason to touch his. He shaved off the inch himself. This trick comes from an old Indian folktale about Birbal, the clever advisor at an emperor's court, where the suspects are told the thief's stick will grow two inches overnight.

Would you have touched your stick? 👇
