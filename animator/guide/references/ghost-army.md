# Ghost Army: decoy field, sonic half-track, camoufleurs (France / Luxembourg, 1944-45)

Sources looked at (not downloaded):
- Wikipedia "Ghost Army" — replica dummy Sherman at the International Spy Museum: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghost_Army
- Wikipedia "Dummy tank" — inflatable M4 Sherman dummy dressed with branches in a hedge: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dummy_tank
- Image search "ghost army sonic halftrack speakers" (Reddit/Hackaday/Slate/Military Machine thumbnails): M3A1 half-track
  with the 500-pound speaker array, soldiers next to it, rows of dummies in a hangar.
- National WWII Museum, "The Ghost Army: Canvas and Camouflage": https://www.nationalww2museum.org/war/articles/ghost-army-canvas-and-camouflage

## Details that make it read
1. Dummy Sherman: olive drab rubber, every edge rounded and slightly puffy; the hull is a fat rounded box, sloped glacis,
   round cast turret, a long barrel that droops a little. Seams run along the hull in raised lines; a fill valve and a
   rubber hose stub on the back deck. Track run is a flat painted band with puffy road-wheel bumps (no real links).
2. White five-point star on the hull side and turret, a white serial painted on the front.
3. Decoys are dressed with cut branches and half-covered with camouflage net, imperfectly, so aerial recon sees them.
4. Inflated with gas-powered air compressors on a small two-wheel cart, hose snaking across the grass.
5. Sonic half-track (M3A1): flat cab with a canvas top, front wheels and rear tracks; on the bed a big square speaker
   array — a 3x3 grid of square horn cells in a dark frame — that can turn toward the enemy. 500 lb.
6. Spoof radio: field radio set on a truck table, Morse key, headphones, a dial lamp.
7. Camoufleurs (603rd): many were art-school artists; sketchbooks, paint pots, stencils for the white star.
8. Country: open French / Luxembourg fields, hedgerows (bocage) between them, rows of poplars, haystacks, a village
   church steeple on the far ridge (an observation post).

## Palette
Olive drab #5d6532 / #4c5428, white star #f2f0e4, grass greens #7f9a48, hedgerow dark green #3e5a30, stone steeple
#b8b0a0, dusk violet, night navy with pale moonlight.

## Strong lights for puppets
Low dawn / sunrise sun (warm side light), the radio dial lamp and the tent lantern (point lights), night: moonlight only
(cool, weak), flares over the decoy field (white-gold point light from above).
