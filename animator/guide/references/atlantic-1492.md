# Atlantic 1492: Columbus's ships, deck and cabin (what-if-columbus)

Sources (read, nothing downloaded):
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Santa_Mar%C3%ADa — nao, ~19 m hull, beam ~5.5 m, three small masts, single main deck, crew ~40.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinta_(ship) and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ni%C3%B1a_(ship) — caravels, 15-18 m on deck, smaller and lower than the nao; the Niña was re-rigged with square sails in the Canaries.
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voyages_of_Christopher_Columbus — departure from La Gomera, the crossing, the 2 a.m. sighting from the Pinta.
- Replica photos (Wikimedia Commons categories "Santa María (replica)", "Niña (replica)", "Nao Victoria").

Details that make it read (the ones that survive a 9:16 frame are starred):
1. * Tubby, dark tarred hull with a pale wale stripe; the sheer rises sharply to a high square sterncastle and a smaller forecastle.
2. * Big square main course and fore course, cream canvas, a red cross painted on the main course (the replicas' look).
3. * A triangular lateen sail on the mizzen mast at the stern.
4. * A round crow's nest (top) on the mainmast, rim about waist height on a man.
5. Bowsprit poking forward with a small spritsail; shrouds and ratlines climbing to the top in a ladder of rope.
6. * Stern lantern on the sterncastle rail, lit at night.
7. Deck: worn planks with tar seams, a hatch grating, a capstan, water barrels lashed by the mast, coiled rope, the high rail (bulwark) with scuppers.
8. Cabin under the sterncastle: low beamed ceiling, small stern windows with leaded panes, a hanging lantern, a chart table.
