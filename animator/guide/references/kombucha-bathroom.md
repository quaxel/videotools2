## What makes it read as this place
- A white enamel roll-top bathtub on four claw feet, chrome taps and a hand shower at one end.
- Brewing: big glass jars of amber tea with a pale beige disc (the culture) floating on top, the mouth covered with a cloth tied by string; flip-top bottles lined up.
- Wall tiles to shoulder height (square, green), a towel on a rail, a small mirror cabinet, a plant on the sill.
- Here the tub itself is full of brown-amber tea with the culture floating, a ladle and a thermometer.

## Palette
Bottle green tiles (#3c8a5a), amber-brown tea (#a8641e), white enamel, cream culture (#e8d9b0).

## Light for puppets
A bare bulb over the mirror: warm point light, moderate.
