# Café glass door — visual references

Looked at (not copied; translated into the kit's cel style):
- Café shopfronts and doors — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=cafe+storefront+glass+door&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image
- Café door types: slim steel/aluminium frames, glass bands, lever handles, door closers — https://www.studiomatrx.org/guides/cafe-door-india
- Storefront door with push bar and pull (model reference) — https://embed-3dwarehouse-classic.sketchup.com/model/3fda60f7428eecafb909a01631a07f78/Storefront-Door

## What makes it read as this place
- Full-height single glass door in a slim black (or satin aluminium) metal frame, wider bottom rail, flanked by big fixed shop-window panes with the same frame.
- Horizontal round push bar across the inside at waist height; a vertical bar-pull on the outside; a door closer arm at the top corner.
- Hanging OPEN sign on a small chain from a suction hook, red/white lettering, swings when the door moves.
- Glass decals: frosted band at eye height, small opening-hours vinyl block and a painted cup/steam motif (no logos).
- Painted signage fascia band over the door; a little bell or chime on a curled bracket at the top inside.
- Through the glass: pavement, a kerb, a parked bicycle, opposite shopfronts, a street tree; reflections lie diagonally across the pane.
- Doormat inside, a chalk A-frame sign outside on the pavement.

## Palette
Frame black (#1e2124), morning glass tint (#bcd9e8), street blue-grey (#7f97ab), sky (#cfe4f2), OPEN red (#d23b33), warm café interior wood (#9a6a43) as the only warm note.

## Light for puppets
Cool blue morning daylight through the glass is the strong source: a soft cool key from the street side. Interior pendants are weak warm fill on the inside face only.
