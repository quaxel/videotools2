## What makes it read as this place
- The oven sits **built into a tall cabinet column** at chest height, or under the worktop; stainless front, dark
  glass door with a horizontal bar handle.
- Above the door, a **control strip**: two round knobs left and right and in the middle a small black display with
  glowing 7-segment digits (blue, green or orange), a little clock icon.
- Plain handle-less cabinets in a light colour, a wooden worktop, a tiled splashback, a kettle and a plant.
- The oven's plug and cable run into a wall socket; radio alarm clocks have chunky red LED digits.

## Palette

orange (#ff9f1c) or green (#39ff7a), terracotta wall (#e07a3a).
