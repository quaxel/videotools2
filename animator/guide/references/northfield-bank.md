# Northfield First National Bank, 1876 (reference notes)

Sources looked at: Northfield Historical Society (northfieldhistory.org/1876-bank-raid, the bank restored to its 1876 look in the Scriver Building), Clio entry with the 1876 interior photo (theclio.com/entry/88004), Northfield History Collaborative (bank minutes: the directors bought "a burglar proof safe with a time lock").

- Small lobby: a long dark wooden counter, chest high, with a carved front and a brass/iron teller grille on top; panelled wainscot, cream plaster above, a wall clock, gas/oil lamps.
- The vault opens behind the counter: a heavy iron door on big hinges in a masonry wall; inside, an iron safe on the floor.
- Safes of the period: dark green or black iron painted with gold lines and lettering (the bank's name), a brass handle and a combination dial; the time lock is a clockwork box (one to three movements) fixed to the inside of the door, sometimes behind a small glass window.
- Outside: two-storey stone/brick commercial block (the Scriver Building), arched windows; in the set the western false-front BANK on the right stands in for it.
