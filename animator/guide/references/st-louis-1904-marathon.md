## The road (country stretch)

- A pale, rutted dirt road, two wheel ruts, between dry summer grass verges; dust hangs in the air and rolls up
  behind every car (big, warm-grey cel dust clouds — the defining feature).
- Split-rail and post-and-wire fences, a few big oaks and elms, telegraph poles with cross-arms along the road,
  a farm in the distance; low rolling hills fading into hazy heat.
- Hard white sun high overhead, bleached sky (pale blue to white near the horizon). Heat shimmer.

## The car (1904 open touring car, no brand)

- Tall and short: a boxy hood in front with a brass radiator and two brass lamps, an open tonneau with two rows of
  tufted leather bench seats, a steering wheel on a raked column, big wooden-spoke wheels with thin tyres, curved
  mudguards, a running board. Deep red or dark green body with brass trim. Driver in a cap and duster coat.
- Breakdown: steam/smoke from the hood, a lurch, a wheel wobble.

## The finish (Francis Field)

- A cinder track (warm reddish brown) with white lane chalk, a finish line and a finish tape held between two
  posts; a long low wooden grandstand packed with spectators: men in dark suits and straw boaters or bowlers,
  women in white blouses and wide hats; bunting (red, white, blue) along the grandstand rail.
- An officials' table at the line with a results board (chalkboard on an easel): runner names with times; this is
  where DISQUALIFIED gets chalked.

## People

- Runners: white sleeveless singlet with a big black number bib (Lorz 31, Hicks 20 — not shown on screen as
  facts), dark or white shorts to mid-thigh, socks, black leather shoes. Lorz: dark hair. Hicks: moustache-free,
  thin, dark hair; at the end held up by two men in white shirts, dark trousers and hats.
- Officials: dark suits, waistcoats, bowler hats or boaters, a megaphone.
