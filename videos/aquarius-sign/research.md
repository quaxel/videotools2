# An Aquarius Quits When It Gets Cool

Satire. The video makes no factual claims: it exaggerates the popular astrology stereotype of Aquarius
(independent, eccentric, a rebel and humanitarian who keeps people at a distance, and drops anything the moment
it becomes mainstream). Every number in the script (three days, five people, a million views, five fans, one
switch) is a story number listed in plan.json -> story_numbers.
