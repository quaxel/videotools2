## What makes it read as this place

- **The river front:** one long, low, symmetrical Gothic front in warm sand-coloured limestone, rows of tall narrow
  window bays divided by thin buttresses that rise into pinnacles above the parapet; a stone terrace wall drops
  straight into the water.
- **The two towers:** the massive square Victoria Tower at the south end (pyramid cast-iron roof, flagstaff) and the
  slim Clock Tower at the north end with its big clock faces and a tall gilded spire. In 1858 the building is still
  being finished: a little scaffolding on one tower reads right.
- **The water:** wide, brown-green and opaque, low in the heat with mud banks showing; brick sewer arches in the
  embankment spill brown water; scum, cabbage leaves and floating rubbish turn with the tide.
- **The boats:** flat-bottomed barges with a square swim-head bow, big leeboards on the sides and rust-red spritsails;
  open lighters worked with long sweeps carrying sacks.
- **Inside:** oak-panelled committee rooms with tall Gothic windows on the river, a long table with green cloth,
  quills, inkwells and papers; curtains hung wet with chloride of lime (shown as decoration only); a wall
  thermometer reads the heat.
- **Crossness:** a Romanesque brick engine house with richly painted cast ironwork (red, green, gold), huge beam
  engines, and outside a vast covered reservoir with iron sluice gates and penstock wheels onto the river.

- **Sewers:** the intercepting sewers are egg-shaped brick tubes (narrow end down), and the Board of Works marked
  them with black cast-iron plaques in white raised capitals: a natural carrier for the 82 MILES label.
- **Lighters in paintings:** low black hulls heaped with cargo, a man at a long sweep, smoke and haze over the river;
  the barges' tan-red spritsails lean steeply.
- **Crossness Octagon:** lacy cast iron painted deep red, green and gold, columns with leaf capitals.

## Palette

Sand limestone and gold for Parliament, brown-olive water with pale scum, hazy yellow-white summer sky, rust-red
sails, oak and green baize inside, dusk orange-violet for the turning tide, sewer brick orange under lanterns, Crossness
ironwork red and green against cold blue-grey water.

## Strong lights

The low hazy sun outside (warm key), the dusk sun behind Parliament (orange rim), oil lanterns in the sewer tunnel,
the open window in the committee room (daylight from the river side).
