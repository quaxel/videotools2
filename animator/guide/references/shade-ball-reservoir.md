## What makes it read as this place
- A **flat black surface to the horizon**: millions of glossy black spheres packed edge to edge; each catches a
  small white highlight from the sky, so the field reads dark grey with sparkle, not pure black.
- A long **sloped embankment** (pale tan concrete / lined bank) running down into the water at ~30°.
- **Dry brown hills** behind with a line of **power pylons**; hard blue Southern-California sky.
- At releases: **white bulk sacks** on the slope pouring black balls that bounce and roll down; workers in hi-vis.
- A **chain-link fence** along the service road at the top of the slope; a small inlet tower.

## Palette
Sky blue (#7fc1ff), bank tan (#d8c39a), hills ochre (#b98a4a), balls black (#141414) with white highlights,
hi-vis yellow (#e8ff3a) / orange (#ff7a1a).

## Light for puppets (strong sources only)
Hard midday or low afternoon sun from one side: a distant warm light (strength ~1.2). Nothing else strong.
