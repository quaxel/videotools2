# Camera plan — what-if-waterloo

Money shots: **S2** (two armies on two ridges: low behind Napoleon on his grey, French columns going down into the valley, Wellington's red lines and flags on the far ridge, the hero field gun firing), **S4** (rain at night onto the bivouac, crane down through the streaks onto the gun sunk in mud while the light drifts to a wet grey dawn) and **S8** (Blucher rears on his black charger while the Prussian columns pour over the crest in late-afternoon gold). Also strong: S1 (push onto the strawberries with gunfire flickering in the window), S9 (the Guard climbs into the volley smoke under Wellington at dusk).
