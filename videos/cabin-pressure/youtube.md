## Title
Why Feeling GREAT Is the Danger When Oxygen Masks Drop ✈️ #Shorts

## Description
When the oxygen masks drop, the most dangerous thing on the plane is how good you feel.

At cruise altitude the air outside is far too thin to keep you conscious, so an airliner pressurizes its cabin to feel like no more than 8,000 feet. If the cabin loses pressure it can fill with fog, and the masks must drop before the cabin altitude passes 15,000 feet. The real danger is hypoxia: instead of fear, a starving brain often feels euphoria, a warm sense of well-being. At 35,000 feet the FAA's time of useful consciousness is a minute at most. Pulling the mask down fires a chemical oxygen generator that runs for at least twelve minutes, even if the bag never inflates, while the pilots put on their own masks first and dive to 10,000 feet, where the air is breathable again.

Did you know the bag on the mask might never inflate? 👇
