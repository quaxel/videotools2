## What makes it read as this place
- A huge curved or flat **video wall** across the whole back: a dark panel covered in a single-line network
  diagram — thin green, yellow and red lines joined by small square nodes (substations).
- In front, **curved console desks** in one or two rows, each operator facing a bank of 3-6 monitors stacked two high.
- A single large **frequency readout** (digital, big numbers) sits on the wall: the one number everyone watches.
- Dim room, light coming mostly from the screens; ceiling with recessed cool lights or a blue cove.
- Operators in plain shirts with headsets/lanyards, phones on the desk, binders.
- Older rooms: a mimic board with toggle switches and analogue meters (a needle dial for Hz).

## Palette
Deep navy/teal room (#0b1a2a), screen-blue glow (#5ad1ff), network-line green (#39ff7a), warning amber (#ffb000)
and red (#ff3b30), console grey (#3a4652).

## Light for puppets (strong sources only)
The monitor banks right in front of the operators (cool blue-white point light at face height). The ambient room
blue does not change them. In the alarm re-light, a red rotating beacon.
