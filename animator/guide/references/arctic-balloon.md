## The balloon (Örnen, "the Eagle")
- A near-round envelope ~20.5 m across of varnished silk: in photos it reads very dark (brown-black against the white
  sky), a little taller than wide, with a lighter fabric valve/appendix flap at the bottom.
- A cord net over the top half gathers into a load ring a little above the basket; gear sacks hang round the ring.
- Small, squat cylindrical wicker basket hung a few metres below the ring; three men's heads and shoulders show over the rim.
- Long drag ropes trail from the basket to the ice in a slack bundle (lost at takeoff).
- Down on the ice: the envelope sits on the floe like a dark dome, half deflated, net lines running to the basket lying
  on its side; the men stand small beside it.

## Danes Island station
- An octagonal plank tower, taller than wide, open scaffolding round it, the balloon's top showing above the planks.
- Behind: steep dark mountains streaked with snow, low cloud; in front: snowy rocky slope down to grey-green water.
- A flagpole with the Swedish flag on top.

## Pack ice
- A flat white floe broken by pressure ridges (piled blocks, blue-white faces), open leads of black water,
  pale grey sky, everything washed out in fog. Sledge: low wooden runners, a big square load lashed on top.

## Kvitøya 1930
- A low white dome of ice behind a narrow strip of dark gravel and boulders at the shore; drift ice in grey water.
- The sealing ship: a small wooden motor sealer, dark hull, one mast with a crow's nest, a wheelhouse aft.
- The camp: a canvas-and-wood boat on a sledge half buried in snow, rocks, a few scattered items.
