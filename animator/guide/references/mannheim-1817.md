# Mannheim, Grand Duchy of Baden, 1816-1817 (volcano-bicycle)

Sources read: Wikipedia "Mannheim" (history: a grid of streets "at right angles to each other" (Duff, 1819), the
electoral palace and Jesuit church finished 1760, Baden since 1802); Wikipedia "Karl Drais" and "Dandy horse" (photo
captions: a c. 1820 draisine of cherry wood and softwood; two wheels in line, hinged front wheel and handlebar);
Cycling UK's Draisienne article (two small carriage wheels on a wooden frame, a steering column with an armrest).
Looked at in the browser: the photo of a c. 1820 wooden dandy horse (Wikipedia "Dandy horse" lead image): two big
spoked wheels nearly touching, the beam just over the wheel tops, the padded armrest board on struts in front of the
saddle, the tiller from the front fork; the model's wheelbase was shortened and its wheels enlarged from it. Wikimedia
Commons engravings of the Mannheim Paradeplatz (18th century, "Kupferstich Paradeplatz" search): long rows of two-
and three-storey houses with steep hipped and mansard roofs round a square, a tall tower with a lantern spire. Not
verified: the plaster colours and red sandstone frames (general knowledge of baroque Baden towns). No photo references
for the Tambora, 1816 field, lane or inn sets.

Details that make it read (the 3-4 that fit a 9:16 frame are marked *):
- * Straight streets of two- and three-storey baroque town houses, plastered in cream, ochre, pale rose and pale
  green, with red Neckar sandstone window and door surrounds and corner stones.
- * Steep red-brown tiled roofs, many with dormers; mansard roofs on the grander houses.
- * Tall windows with small panes and painted wooden shutters (green, brown).
- * A church tower with a baroque onion/lantern cap over the roofs (the Jesuit church dome and tower).
- Cobbled streets with a stone gutter, a pump or well, hitching posts and carts.
- Wall-bracket oil lanterns (gas lighting came later).
- Country roads out of town: poplar-lined, flat Rhine plain fields, milestones, coaching inns (Relaishaus) where
  post horses were changed.

Palette: warm plaster creams and ochres, red sandstone, red-brown tile, grey cobbles, green shutters; sky pale blue
in the morning, rose and gold at dusk, slate grey in the overcast 1816 scenes.

Lights strong enough for the puppets: the low morning sun (warm key from behind-left), the dusk sun (rose rim from
the street's end); none in overcast (flat cool fill).
