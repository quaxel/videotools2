## Title
He Told the James Gang the Safe Was Locked. It Wasn't 🔓 | Northfield 1876 #Shorts

## Description
He told eight armed outlaws the bank safe was locked. It wasn't.

On 7 September 1876 the James-Younger gang rode into Northfield, Minnesota, to rob the First National Bank. Acting cashier Joseph Lee Heywood told them the safe was on a time lock, a clockwork that keeps the bolts shut until a set hour. The clock had never been set and the safe was unlocked, but the outlaws believed him and never tried the handle. When one stepped into the vault, Heywood threw the door on him. Teller Alonzo Bunker escaped out the back with a bullet in his shoulder, and outside the town armed itself. The gang fled with $26.70. Three outlaws were killed and three captured, and the gang never recovered. Heywood was killed for refusing.
