## Description

Two sevens… and the third stops one click short. That's not bad luck. It's by design.

The reel you see isn't the reel the machine uses. Inside, a chip picks from a much bigger "virtual reel": the jackpot symbol gets just one hidden spot, while the blanks right next to it get many. So the reels land beside the jackpot far more often than the visible symbols suggest. The odds of winning stay tiny, but near misses come up all the time.

Your brain treats a near miss almost like a win and wants to try again. The machine just counts it as a loss.
