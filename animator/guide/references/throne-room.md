# Throne room (palace, night and dawn) — references

Sources looked at (Wikimedia Commons media search, 2026-11-04):
- "Topkapi palace Arz Odasi interior" — the Audience Chamber: a canopied throne (baldachin on four gilded posts) set in
  a corner, carpeted platform, white walls, arched windows high up, gilded painted ceiling.
- "Topkapi harem tiles hall" — the harem halls: walls covered floor to ~2.5 m in Iznik tiles (white ground, cobalt and
  turquoise ogival medallions and saz leaves), plain plaster or painted domes above, arched doorways framed by tiles,
  grilled windows, low stone benches along the wall, dim light with deep shadows.
- "throne room castle" — European palace throne rooms: a raised throne on a stepped dais, a red canopy, heavy
  doors, tall candelabra, polished stone floor with reflections.

Details that make it read as a palace throne room (pick 3-4 per shot in 9:16):
1. Tile dado: square tiles with a turquoise/cobalt medallion on white, about 1.8 m high round the walls, a dark
   border tile on top; cream plaster above.
2. Canopied throne on a 2-step dais: gilded posts, a domed red-and-gold canopy, a red cushioned seat.
3. Tall arched windows with an iron grille: blue moonlight bars at night, golden shafts at dawn.
4. Big stone floor tiles in a light/dark checker — the guards measure sticks against them (the tile edge is the ruler).
5. Heavy double wooden doors in a tiled arch, a big iron bolt across them.
6. Tall brass candlesticks (1.4 m) with fat candles: the strong light that lights the puppets at night.
7. A low stone bench along the wall (guards sit and sleep on it).
8. The Queen's jewel stand: a velvet cushion on a gold pedestal (empty in the hook).
