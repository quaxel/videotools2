# Research: Why movie theater popcorn is so expensive

## Mechanism

Theaters do not keep most of the ticket price. Distributors (the studios) license each film for a share of
the box office: at AMC's US theaters, film exhibition costs were about half of admissions revenue in 2023,
and industry consultants describe deals that take upwards of 70 percent, highest in a film's opening weeks.
Concession sales are not shared with the studio, and the food itself is cheap: AMC's US food and beverage
costs were 17.4 % of food and beverage revenue. So concessions, a minority of revenue, carry a large part of
the profit, while the whole theater runs on a thin margin. Theaters keep tickets low enough to fill seats and
price popcorn high: the bucket pays for the building.

## Claims

- AMC US markets 2023: film exhibition costs (paid to distributors) were 50.8% of admissions revenues — about half of every ticket (half) — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411579/000141157924000021/amc-20231231x10k.htm
- AMC US markets 2023: food and beverage costs were 17.4% of food and beverage revenues — about seventeen cents of every snack dollar (seventeen) — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411579/000141157924000021/amc-20231231x10k.htm
- AMC: food and beverage sales are its second largest source of revenue after box office admissions — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1411579/000141157924000021/amc-20231231x10k.htm
- "The film industry will charge movie theaters upwards of 70 percent of the box office revenue" (seventy percent) — https://www.marketplace.org/story/2014/08/04/why-does-popcorn-movies-cost-so-much
- Concessions are not shared with the studios; they are about 20 percent of gross revenues (a fifth) but some 40 percent of theaters' profits (forty percent) — https://www.marketplace.org/story/2014/08/04/why-does-popcorn-movies-cost-so-much
- Whole-theater profit margins average around 4.3 percent industry-wide per IbisWorld (about four cents per dollar, four) — https://www.marketplace.org/story/2014/08/04/why-does-popcorn-movies-cost-so-much

Notes: the 70 percent figure is a consultant's description of deal terms (2014), not an average. AMC's 50.8 % is
the 2023 US average across all films and weeks. Marketplace's 20/40 split and 4.3 % margin are industry-wide estimates.
