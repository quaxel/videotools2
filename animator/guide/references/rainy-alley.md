## What makes it read as this place

Alley (Omoide Yokocho)
- Very narrow (2-3 m): low shopfronts crowd one side, an older plain wall the other. Deep perspective to a bright
  end where tall buildings rise.
- Shopfronts: short cloth curtains (noren) over each door, warm yellow interiors spilling out, wooden stools at a
  counter in the doorway, a fat red paper lantern (chochin) with black vertical writing at a corner.
- Tall white vertical signboards with black brush lettering hang off the buildings *perpendicular* to the alley,
  so they face down it, one behind the other.
- Overhead: a mess of sagging black cables, a utility pole with a crossarm and a transformer can.
- The plain side: a row of round paper lanterns along the top of the wall (white, cream, pale blue, each with a
  red band), a red-painted pipe or post, a hanging fern, AC units, a blue net.

Ramen stall (yatai, Nakasu)
- A wooden cart with a counter on three sides, short red-topped round stools in front of it.
- A low tarp roof on four poles; a strip of noren hanging from the roof edge above the customers' heads.
- A bare fluorescent tube under the roof: a hard white light spilling onto the customers — the strongest light
  in the scene. A tall red lantern with "ラーメン" written down it beside the stall.
- Steam rising from a big stock pot at the back of the counter.

Rain at night (Shinjuku)
- Wet ground mirrors every light as a long vertical streak of its colour, stretched toward the viewer.
- Clear umbrellas; stacked vertical neon signs climbing the buildings at the end of the street; near-black sky.
- Rain shows as thin bright streaks only in front of lights; ripples in the puddles.

## Palette
Near-black blue night (#0a0a16), warm lantern red (#ff4a3a), interior amber (#ffc062), fluorescent white
(#fff6e0), neon magenta / cyan / green at the far end, wet asphalt (#1c1b26) carrying all of them as reflections.

## Light for puppets (strong sources only)
The stall's fluorescent tube (white, strongest), the red lantern beside it, and a car's headlights when it
creeps down the alley toward the camera.
