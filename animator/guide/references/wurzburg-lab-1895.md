# Röntgen's laboratory, Würzburg, 1895 (xray-hand)

Sources looked at (Wikimedia Commons, 2026-11-10):
- "Labor von W.C. Röntgen Entdeckerlabor.jpg" (Stephan Popp, CC0): the preserved discovery room in the Würzburg
  memorial, seen full size.
- The institute building (black-and-white facade photo, same search): a three-storey stone block with tall windows.
- Commons media search "Crookes tube Hittorf" (photos of Hittorf / Crookes tubes running and unlit).
- Commons media search "Ruhmkorff induction coil" (two photos of 19th-century coils).
- Röntgen's paper in translation (web.lemoyne.edu/GIUNTA/Roentgen.html) for the black paper shield and the screen.

What the photos show (and what reads in a 9:16 frame):
- Off-white plaster walls, tall windows with white frames, small panes and pale curtains drawn to the sides; a dark
  wooden glass-fronted cabinet against the wall; framed pictures hung high.
- Herringbone parquet floor in honey oak.
- Long narrow work tables of dark brown wood on slim, slightly tapered legs with a low stretcher; a three-legged wooden
  stool with a round seat.
- The induction coil: a fat horizontal cylinder (white/cream insulation, here also dark) between two round end plates,
  on a polished red-brown wooden base, a brass interrupter at one end and two discharger rods with ball ends on top.
- The Hittorf tube: a long, conical glass bulb lying on its side, wide rounded end, narrow neck with a metal electrode
  wire, a short side arm pointing down near the neck for the second electrode; held by a wooden clamp on a stand. When
  it runs, the wide end glows green and the neck blue-violet. In the dark scenes it is boxed in black cardboard.
- The screen (from the paper): a sheet of paper painted with barium platinocyanide; drawn as a small wooden frame on a
  stand, glowing a bright yellow-green in the dark.

Palette: off-white walls, dark walnut furniture, honey parquet, brass; by lamplight amber; in the dark deep navy with the
screen's phosphor green as the only strong light.

Lights strong enough to light the puppets: an oil lamp (warm point light, lamp shots), the glowing screen (green point
light from the screen face, dark shots), window daylight (day shot).
Pick for the narrow frame: the screen, the boxed tube on its stand, one table with the coil, a window edge or curtain.
