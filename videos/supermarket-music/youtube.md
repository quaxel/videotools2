## Description

That soft supermarket music isn't there for you. It's there for your feet.

In 1982, a researcher switched a supermarket between slow and fast songs. With slow music, shoppers walked slower, and on slow-music days sales were 38% higher than on fast-music days.

Music can even choose what you buy. A wine shop played French accordion music one day and German oompah the next. On French days, 77% of the wine sold was French. On German days, 73% was German. Out of 44 shoppers asked, just one mentioned the music.

The store isn't just playing songs. It's setting your speed.
