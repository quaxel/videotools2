## Description

Your gym makes the most money from the days you don't go.

Economists followed thousands of gym members for three years. New members expected to go about 9 times a month and actually went about 4. On a $70 plan, that's over $17 a visit, when a 10-visit pass cost $10. On average, members lost about $600, and they kept paying for more than two months after their last workout before cancelling.

Gyms aren't selling you workouts. They're selling you the plan to work out.

Would you rather pay per visit or monthly? 👇
