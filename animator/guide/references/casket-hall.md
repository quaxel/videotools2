# Medieval stone hall with the three caskets — references

Sources looked at (Wikimedia Commons media search "castle great hall interior medieval", 2026-11-09): great halls
with ashlar walls and timber roofs (Winchester-type aisled hall, Old Wardour and Dover Castle reconstructions as
described by Historic England), a heavy oak door with iron strapwork, tall pointed windows with deep splays.

Details that make it read as a medieval hall (pick 3-4 per shot in 9:16):
1. Ashlar walls of cream-grey stone blocks with darker mortar, chipped edges and soot stains above torches.
2. A dark timber roof: tie beams and arched braces, the ceiling lost in shadow.
3. Tall pointed (lancet) windows with deep stone splays; daylight shafts falling across the floor.
4. Wall hangings: a long woven hanging behind the dais (deep red with a gold border, no heraldry from a real house).
5. Iron torch brackets / candle stands with fat candles: the strong warm light that lights the puppets.
6. A trestle table with a cloth, the three caskets on it; a raised dais with a carved chair behind.
7. Flagstone floor with rushes scattered near the walls.
8. Heavy oak door with iron straps and studs.

Palette: stone #cbbfa6 / #9a8d76, mortar #6e6454, timber #4a3020, hanging red #8a1f22, gold #e0b040, flagstones
#8a8070 / #6a6256, candle light #ffb860.
Light: candles and torches (warm points, flicker) light the puppets; the window shaft is a cool-white distant light.
No real coats of arms, no lettering on the hangings.

## Tournament lists (same video)

Sources: Wikimedia Commons "jousting tournament reenactment" (reenactment photos: wooden tilt barrier with painted
posts, sand or dirt lists, white and striped round pavilions, spectator stands with coloured rails, pennants).
1. The tilt: a long low wooden barrier, planks painted green with red-and-white posts.
2. Sand/dirt lists churned into mud puddles; straw at the edges.
3. Round pavilions with conical tops, striped red/white and blue/white, a pennant on each pole.
4. A stand with a striped awning and a coloured rail, a crowd of small figures.
5. Trees and a castle wall behind; a blue sky with clouds.
Palette: grass #7aa84a, sand #c8a870, mud #6a5034, tilt green #2e7a4a, posts red #c83a2a / white, pavilion stripes.
Light: bright midday sun (a warm distant light from the right).
