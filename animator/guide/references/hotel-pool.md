# Hotel pool — visual references

Looked at — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=hotel+pool+sun+loungers&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## What makes it read as this place
- Bright turquoise water with a pale tiled edge and a chrome ladder; ripple lines on the water.
- White or striped loungers in a row on a paved deck, a folded towel on each, a big parasol.
- Palm trees and low white hotel blocks behind, blue sky.

## Palette
Turquoise (#2ec4c4), deck cream (#f2e6c8), parasol stripes (white / coral), palm green, sky (#9fd8ff).

## Light for puppets
Bright sun, but ambient here; no extra puppet light.
