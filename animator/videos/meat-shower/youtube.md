## Title
The Day Meat Fell From the Sky in Kentucky 🥩 #Shorts

## Description
In 1876, a strange "meat shower" was reported on a Kentucky farm. The most likely explanation is still unproven.

On 3 March 1876, near Olympian Springs in Bath County, Mrs. Crouch was making soap in her yard under a clear sky when small pieces of what looked like meat began falling around her. Two men later tasted some and said it was mutton or venison. The most cited theory blames vultures throwing up food in flight — but the reports from 1876 never mention any birds, and nobody has proven what really happened.
