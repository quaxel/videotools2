# Commuter train platform — visual references

Looked at in the browser (not copied; no operator names or logos):

- Image search, European station platforms with departure boards — https://www.bing.com/images/search?q=european+train+station+platform+morning+commuters+departure+board
  (stock photos of a large Spanish terminus, a French station hall, a London departure board, a commuter platform
  with a hanging round clock and a split-flap/LED departure board)

## What makes it read as this place
- A long raised **platform edge** with a yellow warning line; the track and the train side right next to it.
- A **round platform clock** hanging from a bracket and a **departure board** (black with amber LED text, rows of
  times and destinations) hanging from the canopy.
- A steel canopy with a glazed roof; benches, a bin, a platform-number sign.
- The train: a long flat side with a row of windows, sliding doors with a red/green door light, a coloured stripe.

## Palette
Cold morning blue (#0a1430 background), concrete (#8a93a0), yellow edge line (#ffd23f), board black with amber text
(#ffb000), train white/teal with a red stripe.

## Light for puppets (strong sources only)
None strong in the morning grey; the train's headlight only when it pulls in. Puppets stay as drawn.

## Picked for a 9:16 frame
1. Round clock + departure board stacked above the puppet.
2. Train side with sliding doors (closing, pulling away).
3. Yellow edge line on the platform floor.
