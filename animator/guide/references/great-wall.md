## What makes it read as this place

The walkway (eye level)
- A paved top 4-5 m wide of big grey stone slabs, worn and uneven, running between two parapets.
- Outer side: a tall crenellated parapet — square merlons with gaps, each merlon pierced by a small square
  loophole. Inner side: a low plain parapet with a flat coping.
- Where the ridge climbs, the walkway turns into steep stone steps up to the next tower.
- Grey-brown brick with lighter mortar lines; tufts of grass in the cracks.

Watchtowers
- Square, two storeys, brick, with a row of small arched windows (three a side) and an arched doorway the walkway
  runs straight through. Crenellated flat top; some carry a small pavilion with a curved grey tile roof.

The landscape
- The wall follows the very crest of the ridges, snaking up and down, with a tower on almost every peak; in the
  distance it is a thin pale line with dots for towers.
- Steep green hills covered in dark shrub dots, rocky patches; behind them range after range of mountains, each
  fainter and bluer (strong aerial haze). Big flat-bottomed white clouds.
