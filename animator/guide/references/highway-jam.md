## What makes it read as this place
- **Rows of cars nose to tail**, each with two glowing **red brake lights**; the far lanes stream away into haze.
- **Steel guardrail** on the verge, a concrete median barrier with a top lip, white dashed lane lines.
- An **overhead gantry** with a green direction sign; tall lamp posts with orange sodium heads.
- Dusk sky: orange at the horizon going to purple; distant hills in layers.

## Palette
Dusk orange (#ff9f5a) to violet (#3b2a6b) sky, asphalt (#3a3f4a), lane white, brake red (#ff3b30),
headlight warm white (#fff1c8), sodium orange (#ffb347).

## Light for puppets (strong sources only)
The brake lights of the car in front (red, from ahead) and oncoming headlights (warm white distant light).
