## Title
The Box That Says "You Deserve It" Is the Trap 🎁 #Shorts

## Description
He just won the tournament, so the gold box promises him exactly what he deserves.

In a medieval tale from the Gesta Romanorum, three caskets wait: gold, silver and plain lead. The gold one reads "Whoso chooseth me, shall find that he deserveth" and holds dead men's bones; silver promises what his nature desires and holds earth and worms; plain lead holds fine gold and precious stones. Our muddy champion grabs the gold. Why does that line work so well on a winner? In 2014 Lisa Cavanaugh's research in the Journal of Marketing Research found that feeling you deserve it, not your mood, best predicts whether people treat themselves.

When did "you deserve it" last talk you into something? 👇
