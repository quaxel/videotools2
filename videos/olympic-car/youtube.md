## Title
The Olympic Marathon Runner Who Took a Car 🚗 | 1904 St. Louis #Shorts

## Description
He crossed the finish line first… after riding in a car.

At the 1904 Olympic marathon in St. Louis, runners faced a hot, dusty course with only one real water stop. Fred Lorz dropped out, got a ride in a car, and when it broke down he ran the final stretch — and crossed the line first to cheers. Then officials learned about the ride and disqualified him. The real winner, Thomas Hicks, was so exhausted his support team had to hold him up at the finish.

First across ≠ winner. 🏅
