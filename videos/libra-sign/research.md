# Research: Why your Libra sign is secretly wrong

## Mechanism

Astrological sign dates are a fixed calendar (Libra: about September 23 to October 22) set when the zodiac was
mapped over 2,000 years ago. The sky has moved since: Earth's axis wobbles (precession) through one full circle in
about 26,000 years, so the equinox points slide about one degree along the ecliptic every 72 years (30 degrees,
one whole sign, in about 2,160 years). Today the Sun stands in front of the constellation Virgo from about
September 16 to October 30 and only enters Libra around October 31. Libra itself is a late addition: the stars were
Chelae, the Scorpion's Claws, until the Romans cut them off as a separate constellation, the Scales; its two
brightest stars are still called the Northern Claw and the Southern Claw.
