# A Pisces Already Planned the Wedding

Satire. The video makes no factual claims: it exaggerates the popular astrology stereotype of Pisces
(dreamy, kind, romantic, very emotional, living in a fantasy world, falling in love at once). Every number in the
script (one smile, three children, one evening) is a story number listed in plan.json -> story_numbers.
