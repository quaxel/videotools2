## Title
Did Strawberries Lose Napoleon Waterloo? 🍓 What If Grouchy Marched #Shorts

## Description
Napoleon's best chance at Waterloo may have been sitting at breakfast, eating strawberries.

On 17 June 1815, a day before Waterloo, Napoleon sent Marshal Grouchy with about 33,000 men after the Prussians he had beaten at Ligny on 16 June. After a night of heavy rain, the battle opened late, around 11:30. Grouchy, the story goes, was eating strawberries at a notary's house in Walhain when the guns were heard. General Gérard urged him to march to the guns; Grouchy kept to his orders and fought Thielmann's rear guard at Wavre, while most of the Prussians marched west and reached Plancenoit, on Napoleon's flank, in the late afternoon. The Imperial Guard's last attack failed and the French army fled. Wellington called it "the nearest run thing you ever saw in your life". Some historians say Grouchy would have arrived too late anyway, and with the Austrian and Russian armies already on the move, most think Napoleon would still have lost, just somewhere else.

Would one marshal's choice have changed history? 👇
