# Sarajevo, Appel Quay and the Latin Bridge corner, 28 June 1914

Set: `src/videos/what-if-sarajevo/SarajevoSet.tsx` (per-video; move to the kit if reused).

## Sources
- Wikipedia, Latin Bridge: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_Bridge (stone Ottoman bridge, four arches on three
  piers, two round relieving "eyes" above the piers, pavements on consoles added under Austria-Hungary; it crosses
  the Miljacka; its north end, on the Appel Quay, is the assassination site).
- Wikipedia, Assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand:
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assassination_of_Archduke_Franz_Ferdinand (Appel Quay along the river, the right turn
  into Franz Joseph Street at the Latin Bridge, Princip in front of a delicatessen on the corner; the Gräf & Stift
  28/32 PS open car with the top folded down; crowds along the quay; the Town Hall reception).
- No photos were opened for this set (text sources only, plus general knowledge of period postcards of the Appel
  Quay): two- and three-storey Austro-Hungarian facades in cream, ochre and pale pink with cornices and tall shuttered windows, shop
  awnings on the ground floor, gas lamp posts, a low stone river wall, hills with houses and minarets behind.

## Details that make it read (pick 3-4 per frame)
1. The low stone parapet of the quay with the shallow, stony Miljacka below it.
2. The Latin Bridge: pale stone, humped, with round "eyes" over the piers.
3. A cream two-storey corner building with a striped shop awning and a DELIKATESSEN sign over the door and the shop
   window (no shop name: the delicatessen's owner is not needed and the sandwich story is a myth).
4. The narrow side street (Franz Joseph Street) running away from the river between facades.
5. Black-and-yellow Habsburg flags and bunting on the facades; a crowd along the kerb.
6. Hills behind the town with small white houses and a slim minaret.
7. Gas lamp posts with a four-sided lantern; cobbles in the side street, packed earth on the quay.

## Palette
Cream / ochre facades, pale stone, terracotta roofs, black-and-yellow flags, olive-green hills, blue-green river,
a deep green open car with brass lamps.

## Lights
Outdoors on a June morning: a high sun from behind-left (the shadow key), a blue sky dome. No strong point sources;
the shots on the shots' frame (the muzzle flash) are a brief white flash, not a light on the puppets.
