# Bank hall with teller windows — visual references

Looked at — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=bank+interior+tellers&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image
(old teller halls with brass grilles and marble counters; modern branches with glass screens and queue posts).

## What makes it read as this place
- A long counter with a stone top and panelled front, split into windows by glass screens with a small slot.
- A sign hanging over each window (TELLER), a small brass grille on older ones.
- Queue posts with a rope in front of the counter.
- Arched window or a vault door in the back wall; a wall clock.
- Deep green and cream, brass details.

## Palette
Bank green (#1f5c45), cream (#f2ead2), brass (#d4a63a), marble (#e8e4da).

## Light for puppets
None strong: ambient only.
