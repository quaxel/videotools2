## What makes it read as this place
- Flower arch at the end of the aisle (round hoop or rounded rectangle) thickly clustered on one side with roses, peonies and hydrangea, trailing greenery, the other side lighter.
- Long white/ivory aisle runner on the floor with loose blush and ivory petals scattered along its edges; low flower markers or lanterns every few rows.
- Two blocks of white chairs in straight rows; chairs on the aisle tied with wide satin/tulle bows with long tails.
- Fairy/string lights draped in shallow swags from the arch apex and across the ceiling, a canopy of warm dots.
- One or two crystal chandeliers hanging from the ceiling centre-line, maybe wrapped in flowers.
- Tall tiered white cake (3-4 tiers) on a draped side table, sugar flowers cascading down one side.
- Sheer white drapes on the walls, tall windows; a dreamy haze/bloom over everything.

## Palette
Ivory (#fbf6f1), blush pink (#f4c9cf), rose (#e391a1), soft gold (#d8b77a), sage leaf (#9fb59a), warm light dots (#ffe6b0).

## Light for puppets
Chandeliers and the string-light canopy together act as a strong warm key from above; tall windows give a soft pink-white fill. Bloom is decorative only.
