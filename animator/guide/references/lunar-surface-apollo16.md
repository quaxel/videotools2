## Details that make it read
1. Pitch-black sky right down to a sharp, smooth horizon; gentle rolling grey hills (no jagged peaks).
2. Regolith: warm-neutral grey, dimpled with bootprints and small craters, a scatter of angular rocks, each with a
   long hard black shadow on one side; rover tyre tracks with a chevron tread.
3. Lunar module: descent stage wrapped in crinkled gold/amber foil with black panels, four splayed legs with round
   footpads; the angular faceted silver-grey and black ascent stage on top with its antennas.
4. Rover parked by the LM: open frame, four mesh wheels with orange-gold fenders, a white umbrella dish antenna on a
   mast, a TV camera at the front.
5. Flag on a pole with a horizontal rod along its top edge, so it stands out stiff with ripples set in it.
6. Suits: white, darkened grey with dust up the shins and knees; the commander (Young) has red bands on the arms,
   legs and helmet shell; the backpack is a white box with a flag patch and a smaller pack on top.
7. Light: one hard low sun, no fill; shadows pure dark; the lit side warm white. Earth high in the sky (drawn bigger).

Palette: sky #000; regolith #8e8a84 lit / #3a3836 shade; foil #c99a3a with #6a4a1a creases; ascent stage #b8bcc0
and #2a2a2c; suits #f1eee7, dust #9a948a, stripe #c8302c.

Strong lights for puppets: the sun only (a distant warm light, low, from behind-left); Earth gives a faint blue
rim in the earthshine shots.

## Mission Control 1972
Dark room, tiered rows of grey-green consoles with small CRT monitors (green-white text) and many tiny coloured
indicator lights; the front wall: a big blue world-map screen with a ground track, a TV screen with the live picture,
red digital clocks above; a flag at the side; a viewing room with warm-lit glass at the back. Controllers in
short-sleeved white or pastel shirts and ties, headsets. Light: cool screen glow on faces, warm overhead spots.
