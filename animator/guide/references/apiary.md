# Apiary (beehives in a meadow) — visual references

Looked at (not copied; translated into the kit's cel style):
- Apiaries and beekeepers at work — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=apiary+beehives+beekeeper&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## What makes it read as this place
- Box hives: stacked wooden boxes (supers) with a telescoping flat lid, a narrow entrance slot at the bottom, standing on a low stand or pallet off the grass.
- Hives painted in faded pastel colours (white, yellow, blue, mint) in a loose row along a hedge or tree line.
- Beekeeper in a white suit with a round-brimmed hat and black mesh veil; a tin smoker with bellows; a frame lifted out showing hexagon comb.
- Long meadow grass and wildflowers, a wooden shed or fence behind, trees at the edge.
- Bees as small dots drifting round the entrances.

## Palette
Honey amber (#f2a72e), warm wood (#c98a3a), pastel hive paint, meadow green (#7fb348), hazy golden sky.

## Light for puppets
Low warm sun (late afternoon): a golden distant light from the side, soft rim.
