# City street protest — visual references

Looked at (not copied; translated into the kit's cel style):
- Street protests with banners and placards — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=street+protest+march+banners+placards&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image
- City streets with shopfronts — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=city+street+shopfronts+pedestrians+sidewalk&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## What makes it read as this place
- Hand-painted cardboard placards on wooden sticks, held above the heads; a long cloth banner stretched between two poles across the crowd.
- Flags on poles, a megaphone, a few balloons.
- Metal crowd-control barriers (vertical bars, feet) along the kerb.
- Street with painted lane lines, a kerb and paved sidewalk; 4-6 storey facades with rows of windows, awnings over shopfronts, street lamps.
- The same street on a normal day: café tables under awnings, a tram line, people walking.

## Palette
Protest: red banners (#e0262f), white placards, grey asphalt, warm sandstone facades. Normal day (re-dressed): yellow awnings and outfits (#ffd23f).

## Light for puppets
Open daylight; no strong source (puppets as drawn), except low sun for the yellow street.
