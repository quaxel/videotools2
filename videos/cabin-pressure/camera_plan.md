# Camera plan: cabin-pressure (57 s, 12 shots, 9:16)

Frames are video frames (30 fps). Shot boundaries are in `src/videos/cabin-pressure/shots.ts`; inside a shot use
`beat(line, frac, shotId)` for word frames. Every shot moves the camera with `beatPath` (eases out of the first key,
glides, rests at the last). Net travel is 25-40 % of the frame width. Every shot has a soft foreground object near
the lens (Stage `foreground` + `frameSpot`, never over a face or label), the subject sharp, and the background soft.
The post chain is on (`post`) and the camera is `steady`. Light follows the story: warm, then cold and amber at the
emergency, purple at the pivot, gold at the end.
