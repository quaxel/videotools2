# Meeting room surprise party — visual references

Looked at:
- Conference rooms — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=conference+room&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image
- Balloons and confetti — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=balloons+confetti&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## What makes it read as this place
- A long glossy table with high-backed chairs down both sides, a wall screen at the head, blinds on the window.
- Dark: only the corridor light through the door and the screen's standby glow; shapes hide behind the table.
- Lit: balloon clusters tied by strings to chair backs, a paper banner strung across the wall, streamers, a cake.
- Confetti: small paper squares and curls in five bright colours drifting down; party poppers are tubes with a
  twist base that throw a cone of paper.

## Palette
Dark: navy (#0d1430), door light (#ffe6b0). Lit: sunny yellow wall (#ffd23f), balloons red / blue / pink / green.

## Light for puppets
Dark room: the open door (warm, from behind) and the laptop screen (cool, from below the face). Lit room: none strong.
