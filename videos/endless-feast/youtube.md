## Title
The King Who Banned the Music From Stopping 🥁 #Shorts

## Description
He ordered the music to never stop. Every new drum made his feast more boring.

At his victory feast a Shah decrees the band can never take a break, and his joy fades song by song. A blacksmith brings a plain ring that says "This too shall pass", an old fable told by medieval Persian Sufi poets. The thrown ring snaps a lute string, the hall goes silent, and when the music starts again it feels better than ever. In 2009 Leif Nelson, Tom Meyvis and Jeff Galak found the same thing with TV: people said they would rather skip the ad breaks, yet the breaks made shows more enjoyable, because a break interrupts the way we get used to a pleasure. The effect disappeared for people who barely get used to things.

What do you enjoy more after a break? 👇
