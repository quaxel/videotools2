# Glass-walled office — visual references

Looked at (not copied; translated into the kit's cel style):
- Office interiors with glass walls — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=office+interior+glass+wall&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image
- Conference rooms (long table, leather chairs, wall screen) — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=conference+room&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## What makes it read as this place
- Floor-to-ceiling glass partitions in thin metal frames (mullions every ~1 m), with a frosted band at eye height.
- Desks in a row with a monitor on an arm, keyboard, a mug, a paper tray; task chairs on five-spoke bases.
- A wall clock, a whiteboard or wall planner, a tall potted plant in a corner, ceiling panel lights.
- Grey-blue carpet tiles, pale grey walls, cool daylight from a window band.

## Palette
Cool grey (#c9d2db), steel blue (#5b7a99), glass tint (#bfe3f0, 35 % opacity), white desks, one red accent.

## Light for puppets
Only the monitor glow when a face is close to it; the ceiling light is ambient (no puppet light).
