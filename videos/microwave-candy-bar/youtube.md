## Title
The Melted Candy Bar That Invented the Microwave 🍫 #Shorts

## Description
The story goes: a candy bar melted in his pocket, and nothing in the room was hot.

Percy Spencer never finished grammar school and taught himself the science. In the war his method turned out 2,600 radar magnetrons a day. The story goes that, standing next to a running magnetron, he found the chocolate bar in his pocket had melted. He held popcorn kernels by the tube and they popped all over the lab; an egg burst. Microwaves make the water in food vibrate, and the friction between molecules is heat. A patent for microwave cooking was filed on October 8, 1945. The first oven, in 1947, stood about six feet tall and weighed about 750 pounds. Twenty years later, in 1967, a countertop model cost 495 dollars.

What is the strangest thing you have ever put in a microwave? 👇
