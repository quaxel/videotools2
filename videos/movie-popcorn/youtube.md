## Description

Nine dollars for popcorn feels like robbery. But that bucket is the reason the theater still exists.

About half of every movie ticket goes straight to the studio that made the film, and some deals take upwards of 70%, especially in opening weeks. What's left barely covers the building, the staff and the giant screen. But the snack counter is different: the studio gets none of it, and the food itself costs about 17 cents of every snack dollar.

So snacks bring in only about a fifth of the money but around 40% of the profit. Even then, a theater keeps only about 4 cents of every dollar. That's why tickets stay low and popcorn stays high.

Do you buy the popcorn, or sneak in your own? 👇

(Figures are averages and industry estimates; actual deals vary by film, week and theater.)
