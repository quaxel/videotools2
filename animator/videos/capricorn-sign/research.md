# A Capricorn Schedules Her Fun

Satire. The video makes no factual claims: it exaggerates the popular astrology stereotype of Capricorn
(ambitious, disciplined, reliable, a workaholic who plans everything, secretly sentimental). Every number in
the script (3:15, five-year, twenty-six, once, two, one hour, October) is a story number listed in
plan.json -> story_numbers.
