# Niagara Falls and the Lake Erie ice jam, March 1848

Sets: `src/three/NiagaraSets.tsx`.

## Sources (looked at in the browser, Wikimedia Commons media search)

- "American Falls dry 1969": the de-watered American Falls; the brink is a broken, stepped edge of grey dolomite slabs,
  a huge talus heap of jumbled square blocks at its foot, dark wet rock, small pools, the city skyline behind.
- "Horseshoe Falls Niagara from Goat Island": aerial and side views; the crest curves in a deep U, a smooth emerald
  tongue of water over the brink, a white curtain below, a tall mist cloud boiling up out of the plunge pool;
  Goat Island between the falls, a forested flat top on a brown-grey cliff; dark teal water in the gorge below.
- "Niagara River upper rapids": 19th-century engravings and photos; the river above the falls is wide and shallow,
  rows of white standing waves over flat rock ledges, low tree-lined banks, mills and a few buildings on the bank.
- "river ice jam": slabs of ice 30-60 cm thick, tilted on edge and piled in ridges, white tops, blue-grey broken
  edges, open dark water where the jam ends.

## Details that make it read

1. The crest: a long smooth green lip of water that turns white as it falls; the curtain hides the cliff.
2. Mist: a big rolling white cloud rising from the foot of the falls, higher than the crest, with a rainbow in sun.
3. Gorge: dark grey-brown layered cliff walls topped with trees; teal water with foam streaks below.
4. Upper rapids: shallow ledges, rows of white standing waves, all the water leaning toward the brink.
5. Dry: flat grey dolomite ledges in steps, jumbled square blocks, puddles reflecting the sky, wet dark streaks,
   a last thin trickle over the brink; the gorge floor full of fallen blocks.
6. A water-driven mill on the bank: timber-frame mill with a big wooden wheel in a flume.
7. Ice: thick white slabs tilted and stacked, blue edges, packed into a ridge across the river mouth.
8. Period people: 1840s dress, wide hats, long skirts and shawls; lanterns and torches at night.

## Lights strong enough to light puppets

Low sun at dawn (warm side light), the miller's lantern and the torches on the bed (warm point lights), the full
moon only as a cool rim.
