## What makes it read as this place
- Tank: thick glass box with a black plastic rim frame top and bottom, sitting on a cabinet stand; normally filled to about 1-2 cm below the frame.
- Lid/light strip: a black hinged hood or a slim LED bar across the top, throwing a bright band down into the water and wobbling caustic lines on the gravel and back wall.
- Inside: sloped gravel bed (higher at the back), a few rocks, tall wavy plants, an air stone sending a column of small round bubbles that grow as they rise and pop at the surface.
- Filter: a hang-on-back box over the rear rim with a little waterfall lip pouring back in, plus an intake tube with a slotted strainer; cords looped in a drip loop to the socket.
- Overflow: water bulges a hair over the rim (surface tension), then pours as a smooth clear sheet hugging the outside of the glass, breaking into ropes and drips at the bottom frame; splash crowns where it hits, spreading puddles with bright rim highlights on the floor.
- Basement/plumbing room: painted pipes along the wall, an old round dial gauge (white face, black numbers, red needle, brass bezel) on a pipe tee, a brass tap dripping into a puddle, a wall-mounted cone speaker on a bracket.
- Inside-her-head touch: tank glass reads like a giant window, soft vignette, bubbles drift past camera.

## Palette
Deep teal (#0e5c63), sea blue (#1b6fa0), shallow aqua (#4fc3c8), foam white (#e8fbfb), gravel (#c7b48f), plant green (#3f8f5a), pipe grey-green (#5f7470), brass (#b8933f), gauge red (#d23b33).

## Light for puppets
The tank light strip is the strong source: a cool aqua key from above-behind the glass, with caustics on faces near the tank. In the plumbing room, a single bare bulb is the key; puddle reflections are decorative only.
