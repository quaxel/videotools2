## Details that read as the place
1. Shelves of small packs behind clear, slightly blue-tinted acrylic doors, full width of the bay.
2. A yellow shelf-edge strip with white price tags on every shelf, and yellow sale tags sticking out.
3. A red "PRESS FOR HELP" button / call box on the case frame, with a small black keypad lock.
4. Blister packs hanging on metal pegs (the cheap handles stay outside the case, open to grab).
5. White fluorescent ceiling light, grey-white vinyl floor tiles, cool white bay frames.
6. Handle packs are bigger and brighter than the small refill packs.

## Palette
White frames, red accents (call button, sale signs), yellow tags, clear blue acrylic; floor pale grey.

## Lights
Flat fluorescent only: nothing strong enough to light the puppets (mood light).

## Bathroom
Small vanity: white basin, round or rectangular mirror over it, a bar of vanity bulbs above the mirror (the only
strong-ish light, warm white, from above-front), teal or white tile, a towel ring, foam can on the sink edge.
