## Title
A 4-Foot Jump on the Moon Nearly Killed an Astronaut 🌕 #Shorts

## Description
On the Moon, a jump of just four feet nearly killed a man, and his own backpack did it.

April 1972, Apollo 16. At the end of their last moonwalk, Charlie Duke and John Young held their own "Moon Olympics", because the Munich Olympics were coming that year. On the Moon you weigh about one sixth as much, so Duke made a big push and got about four feet high. But as he straightened up, the weight of his backpack pulled him over backward, and he landed right on it. That backpack was a fiberglass shell holding all his life-support systems: had it broken, he would have lost his air. Young's verdict over the radio: "Charlie! That ain't very smart." The pack held. The Moon cuts your weight, not your mass.

Would you have tried the Moon high jump? 👇
