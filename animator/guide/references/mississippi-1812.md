# The Mississippi at New Madrid, 1811-1812

Sets: `src/three/RiverSets.tsx`. Used by `src/videos/mississippi-backward`.

## Sources

- 1851 woodcut of the New Madrid earthquake (Wikimedia Commons, File:New_Madrid_Erdbeben.jpg): log cabins with
  shake roofs tipping and caving in, a crack opening across the ground, people running with arms up, trees whipping
  over in a storm sky.
- Wikipedia, Flatboat: a rectangular flat-bottomed "broadhorn", square ends, a plank cabin over most of the deck,
  a long steering sweep at the stern and side sweeps; a one-way boat floating downstream with the current.
- USGS summary of the 1811-1812 sequence: high banks caving into the river, sand bars and island points giving way,
  trees bent until their branches intertwined, the Reelfoot scarp and the uplift across the river's path.
- History.com (7 Feb 1812): brown water, whirlpools, waterfalls in the river, many boats overwhelmed.
- Wikipedia, Reelfoot Lake: a shallow swampy lake of bald cypress standing in the water, with open basins.

Note: only the woodcut image loaded in the browser (Commons image hosting returned errors); the flatboat and
Reelfoot details come from the article text and general knowledge of the subjects. Re-check against photos when the
image host works.

## Details that make it read

1. A very wide, muddy brown-olive river; the far bank is a low dark line of forest, never mountains.
2. Steep cut banks of brown clay on the outside of bends, with tree roots hanging out; pale sand bars on the inside.
3. Bottomland forest of tall cottonwoods and sycamores (pale trunks), cane underneath.
4. Flatboat: plank box hull, low gabled plank cabin, a sweep oar at each side and a long one at the stern,
   a lantern at night, barrels and a cow pen on the open bow deck.
5. New Madrid: a few hewn-log cabins with shake roofs and stick-and-clay or stone chimneys, rail fences, stumps.
6. The quake: ground cracks, sand blows (sand and water fountaining out of cracks), chimneys toppling, cabins racking.
7. The uplift: the riverbed bulges into a ridge across the channel, the water piles against it and pours over in a
   white waterfall; downstream of the fall the river runs back the way it came.
8. Reelfoot Lake: flared cypress trunks and knees standing in still water, dead grey snags from the drowned forest.

## Palette

River brown-olive #7a6a44 / #5e5236, clay bank #9a6a42, sand bar #e2cf9a, cottonwood green #5f7a3a, sycamore
bark #d8d2c0, cabin logs #8a6a48. Pre-dawn sky (the 7 Feb shock came before dawn): navy to lilac; dawn gold for the
aftermath; Reelfoot sunrise pink-gold on mirror water.

## Lights strong enough to light puppets

The flatboat lantern (warm point), a cabin fire / hearth seen through the door, the low dawn sun. Moonlight only
as a cool rim.
