# 1890s general store (New England) — visual references

Looked at (not copied; details translated into the kit's cel style):

- Interior of a general store (Connecticut Historical Society) — https://emuseum.chs.org/emuseum/objects/25609/interior-of-a-general-store
- Herbert O. Maynard General Store, Shrewsbury, Mass. — https://www.historicnewengland.org/explore/collections-access/gusn/195805
- Hall General Store and Post Office, Union, OR, circa 1890 — https://library-archives.eou.edu/items/show/10188
- Del Wood's Country Store (shelves on barrels) — https://wisconsinhistory.org/Records/Image/IM122286
- General store interior with stove, hanging buckets and a barrel, Kansas 1900 — https://scholars.fhsu.edu/harvey/1665

## What makes it read as this place

- A long dark-wood counter with a glass-fronted display case at one end and a brass balance scale on top.
- Floor-to-ceiling wall shelving behind the counter, packed with tins, jars and paper packets in rows.
- Wooden barrels and crates on the plank floor (crackers, flour), goods stacked on top of some.
- A cast-iron pot-bellied stove with a black pipe going up, in the middle of the room.
- Oil lamps hanging from the ceiling; buckets and tools hanging overhead; a pressed-tin ceiling.
- Wide worn floorboards.

## Palette

Dark walnut and honey wood, dull red and green tins, cream paper labels, black iron, amber lamplight.

## Strong lights

The hanging oil lamps (warm amber, from above) and daylight through the front window. In the narrow frame use
the counter + shelf wall + a barrel or two + one hanging lamp; the lamp is the puppets' key light.

# Old Supreme Court Chamber (U.S. Capitol, Court's home 1860-1935) — visual references

- Old Senate Chamber — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Senate_Chamber
- Old Supreme Court Chamber — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Supreme_Court_Chamber
- Photo of the room as the Court's chamber, c. 1902 (Library of Congress) — https://lostnewengland.com/?p=18805

## What makes it read as this place

- Semicircular room under a white coffered half-dome with a skylight.
- A long raised bench for the justices at the front, with high-backed chairs; crimson drapery hung from a
  mahogany valence behind and above it.
- A gilded eagle with spread wings centred above the chief justice's chair.
- Variegated marble Ionic columns carrying a gallery with an iron railing.
- Crimson and gold: red carpet with gold stars, red swags.

## Palette

Crimson, gold, white plaster, mahogany, grey-veined marble.

## Strong lights

Daylight from the skylight above (soft, cool-white, from overhead); nothing strong at puppet level.
