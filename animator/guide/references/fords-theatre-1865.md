## Details that make it read
1. The State Box: boxes 7 and 8 opened into one, upper level on the stage's left (house right), right beside the
   proscenium. A bowed cream-and-gilt front, swags of red-white-blue bunting, striped flags draped down the ends,
   a blue Treasury Guard flag on a staff at the centre, and a framed portrait of George Washington on the centre
   pillar. Red velvet valance and curtains.
2. Inside: a black walnut rocking chair upholstered in red damask (Lincoln's), a high-backed chair and a sofa.
3. The house: horseshoe of two balconies (dress circle and family circle) with cream fronts and gilt mouldings,
   wooden armchairs in the parquet, gas jets along the balcony fronts; the audience in dark coats and coloured
   dresses, the house lights left up during the play.
4. The stage: gas footlights in a row behind a lip at the front, painted wings and a painted drop (a drawing room
   for Act 3 of Our American Cousin), a green baize-like stage floor cloth, a gilt proscenium arch.
5. Behind the box: the dress circle passage, a narrow vestibule with a white door that Booth barred with a pine bar
   wedged against the wall; a chair for the guard by the door; a gas bracket on the wall, patterned wallpaper.
6. Outside: a three-storey brick front with five arched doorways on 10th Street, gas street lamps; the Star Saloon
   next door (south) with a lit bar window.

## Palette per location
- House and stage: amber gaslight, cream and gilt, red velvet, red-white-blue bunting.
- Box passage / corridor: dark teal wallpaper, one amber gas bracket (night); violet (what-if); grey-blue (dawn).
- Stairs to the dress circle: crimson carpet and wallpaper, low light from above, Booth backlit.
- Saloon: olive and brown wood, green glass bottles, warm lamp; blue street through the window.

## Lights strong enough for the puppets
- Footlights (warm, from below the stage lip): light the actor from below and in front.
- Gas brackets in the passage and on the stairs (warm point lights).
- The saloon lamp over the bar; the White House window (candle) and street torches.
