## Title
Why Razor Refills Are Locked Up Like Jewelry 💎 #Shorts

## Description
The razor was a bargain. The refills are guarded like diamonds.

Cartridge razors run on the razor-and-blades model: a handle sells for about $10, while an eight-pack of refills runs close to $40. Inside each refill, the steel costs well under five cents and the plastic three to five cents, so all the materials come to under thirty cents, against a five-dollar shelf price: fifteen to twenty times what the stuff is worth. The throwaway-blade safety razor was patented in 1904, and the blade count has kept climbing, from two blades in the early 1970s to seven by 2017.
