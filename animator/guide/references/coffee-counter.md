## What makes it read as this place
- Chunky two-group espresso machine in brushed steel on the back counter, portafilter handles sticking out front, white cups stacked upside-down on the warming tray on top.
- Conical grinder beside it with a clear bean hopper on top; a knock box and milk jugs at its foot.
- Curved-glass pastry case at the front of the counter, two shelves of croissants and muffins on white trays, tiny price cards.
- Big black chalkboard menu on the back wall in hand-drawn white/coloured chalk lettering, framed in wood.
- Three or four bare filament bulbs hanging at different heights on black cords over the counter.
- Front of the counter in vertical wood planks with a butcher-block top; a squat glass tip jar and a small tablet register on a stand near the end.
- Barista in a canvas/denim bib apron over a T-shirt, small rectangular name tag on the chest; open shelves of mugs and bean bags behind.

## Palette
Walnut wood (#6b4429), honey top (#b07a45), cream cups (#f2e8d8), chalkboard (#2b2b28), brushed steel (#a9adb0), bulb glow (#ffcf7a), coffee brown (#3d2416).

## Light for puppets
The pendant bulbs are the strong source: warm amber key from above-front. Window daylight from the customer side is a weak cool fill only.
