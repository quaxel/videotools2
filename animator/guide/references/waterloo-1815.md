# Waterloo country, June 1815 (FieldSet, src/videos/what-if-waterloo/WaterlooSet.tsx)

Built from general knowledge of the battlefield (period paintings and today's landscape around Mont-Saint-Jean,
La Haye Sainte, Hougoumont and Plancenoit); no image was copied or downloaded.

Details that make it read as itself:
- open rolling Brabant farmland: two low ridges a valley apart, the fields in standing rye and wheat (tall rye near the lens)
- rows of poplars and elms on the skyline, white-washed walled farms with red tile roofs and a dovecote tower
- armies as blocks: French blue coats with white lapels and cross belts, British red, Prussian dark blue, the Guard's tall bearskins; flags (tricolour with an eagle finial, Union flag, Prussian black-and-white)
- banks of white powder smoke lying along the lines; the night before: heavy rain and mud
- villages with a church spire (Plancenoit, Wavre), Waterloo's domed chapel

Palette per mode: morning green-gold, overcast grey-olive, storm blue-grey drifting to grey dawn, smoke olive,
golden late afternoon, dusk orange-red, frost pale blue-white, sunset rose-violet.
Strong lights for puppets: the low sun (golden, dusk, sunset) as a distant key; a campfire in the storm bivouac.
