# Camera plan — what-if-lincoln

Money shots (the ones a viewer screenshots):
1. **S2 — the crane over the house:** from low among the back rows of the packed, gaslit parquet, rising over the
   heads and gas haze, past the balcony fronts, up to the flag-draped State Box where Lincoln rocks beside Mary.
2. **S6 — Booth's silhouette on the crimson stairs:** low at the foot of the stairs, a hard gas light at the top
   behind him; the camera cranes up with the black silhouette climbing into the light.
3. **S8 — the laugh in warm light, then darkness:** the house rocks back laughing in the footlights' glow, a white
   flash high in the State Box over the laughter, and the gas drops to near-black while the camera keeps drifting.
Also: the slow push-in on the empty chair in the dim passage (S1), and the saloon's warm window light against the
blue street (S4).
