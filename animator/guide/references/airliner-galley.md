## What makes the galley read as itself
- **Carts in bays** under a dark worktop: tall brushed-aluminium boxes side by side, each with a recessed handle and
  a small yellow or white placard, slotted in so only their fronts show.
- **Red latches:** a red turn-paddle hangs at the top corner of every cart bay and every locker. They are the
  strongest colour in the galley and say "aircraft galley" at a glance.
- **Upper row:** steel ovens and small storage boxes (standard units) with black round turn-latches, little control
  panels with green displays, coffee makers with a jug under them.
- **Worktop:** a dark grey counter with a raised rail along its front edge; cups and a stack of trays on it.
- **Ceiling:** low, white, with flat strip lights; a small oxygen drop box in the ceiling panel over the working
  area (the galley gets masks too).
- **Jump seat:** a fold-down seat on the wall by the door, grey cushion, a shoulder harness of grey straps crossing
  to a buckle, a handset in a cradle beside it.
- **Curtain:** a navy curtain on a rail separates the galley from the cabin. The floor is dark grey-blue.

## What makes the PSU generator read as itself
- A shallow beige box above the seat row with a hinged drop door, open at the bottom.
- The generator: a **silver steel cylinder** lying on its side, held by two metal straps, with a coloured band near
  one end and a printed label; at one end the **firing mechanism** (a spring-loaded striker held back by a pin).
- **Lanyards** run from each mask down from the pin: pulling any mask pulls the pin, the striker fires, and the
  canister starts making oxygen. It gets very hot; a red heat-warning tag or cover sits beside it.
- **Yellow cups** with clear, limp reservoir bags and clear tubes, folded under the canister until the door opens.

## Light for puppets
- **Galley:** the ceiling strip lights are the strong source, a cool white from straight above and slightly in
  front; in the emergency an amber caution light adds a warm side light.
- **Generator shot:** the canister's orange glow lights the inside of the box only; Barry below is lit by the cabin
  windows, with a faint warm hint from above.

## Chosen for a 9:16 frame
1. Galley: a row of aluminium carts with red latches behind Sofia at waist height, ovens and lockers above her head,
   a yellow mask on a taut tube from the ceiling drop box in her fist.
2. A soft cart corner close to the lens on one edge; the jump seat and curtain at the far edge for depth.
3. Generator: the silver canister big in the frame, the pin and lanyard at its end, the label "O2 GENERATOR" facing
   the lens, the orange glow and heat shimmer above it, then a crane down the tube to Barry below.
