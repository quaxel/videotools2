## Title
They Flew a Balloon to the North Pole and Vanished 🎈 | 1897 #Shorts

## Description
In 1897, three men tried to fly a balloon to the North Pole. They weren't found for 33 years.

On 11 July 1897, the Swedes S. A. Andrée, Nils Strindberg and Knut Frænkel lifted off from Danes Island, Svalbard, in the hydrogen balloon Örnen ("the Eagle"). It was a varnished silk balloon with no engine, meant to be steered by long drag ropes trailing on the ice. Right after takeoff about 530 kg of those ropes fell away. Then fog and rain froze on the envelope, and after 65 hours the balloon came down on the pack ice, far short of the pole. For about three months they hauled heavy sledges across about 400 km of broken, drifting ice, until they reached White Island (Kvitøya) in October 1897. Then nothing. In 1930, Norwegian seal hunters on the Bratvaag landed on the island and found their boat, the men and their diaries. Strindberg's film was there too, and 93 of its frames could still be developed: the last pictures of three men who never came home.
