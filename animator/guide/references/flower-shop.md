- Florist shop interiors — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=florist+shop+interior&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image
- Florist layout (buckets on floor and tables, counter splitting front/back, cooler) — https://bizfluent.com/13426272/how-to-design-a-floral-shop-layout
- Tiered bucket stands (3-4 tiers, 12-16 buckets, on wheels) — https://shop.scope.org.uk/products/flower-stand-3-tier-flower-display-stand-12-buckets-gold

## What makes it read as this place
- Stepped stands (3-4 tiers) of tall zinc or plastic buckets, each crammed with one kind of cut flower; more buckets straight on the floor in front.
- Wrapping counter in timber with big rolls of kraft paper and tissue on a wall-mounted roller bar, spools of ribbon on a dowel, scissors and twine on top.
- Walk-in style cooler with glass doors along one wall: shelves of buckets and ready bouquets, misted glass, a faint cold glow.
- Hanging plants in macramé or wire baskets from the ceiling, trailing pothos down the shelves.
- Green painted walls or a foliage wall, wooden crates and potted plants on rustic shelves.
- Small chalk price tags on sticks in the buckets; a watering can, a hose and a wet concrete floor patch.

## Palette
Leaf green (#4e8a4a), deep foliage (#2f5a36), sage wall (#a7c3a0), kraft paper (#c9a074), zinc bucket (#9aa4a6), flower pops: pink (#f08cae), yellow (#f5d04e), white (#fbf8f0).

## Light for puppets
Daylight from the shop window is the strong source: a soft neutral-cool key from the front. The cooler glow is decorative only.
