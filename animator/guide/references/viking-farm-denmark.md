# Viking farm, Denmark, c. AD 900-980 (viking-vs-roman: Bjorn's farm)

Sources (read 2026-11-06):

  https://www.ribevikingecenter.dk/en/explore/viking-farmstead ,

## Details that make it read as a Danish Viking farm
1. **Boat-shaped longhouse**: long walls bow outward (Fyrkat: ~5 m wide at the ends, ~7.5 m in the middle, 28.5 m
   long), so the roof ridge and eaves curve too. Low walls, a big roof.
2. **Leaning outer posts** along the long walls, braced against the wall like buttresses (Fyrkat type).
3. **Walls**: oak posts with horizontal planks between them, or wattle and clay daub (pale grey-ochre clay).
4. **Roof**: reed thatch (Fyrkat farm) or oak shingles (Hviding). We use thick thatch with a heavy eave line and a
   smoke hole near the hearth; the same roof in every scene.
5. **Doors in the long side**, near the ends, with a small porch; storerooms at the short ends.
6. **Wattle (woven) fences** round the yard: stakes with withies woven between them (Hviding: 3 km of it).
   Not post-and-rail. The story's gate is a framed plank gate hung on posts in that wattle fence.
7. **Outbuildings**: a light wattle barn with thatch, a separate stable, a plank smithy set apart, pit houses.
8. Yard life: woodpile and chopping block, buckets, a cloth on a line, hens, sheep in a pen, trodden mud at the gate.
9. Landscape: low, green, gently rolling; fields and meadow; a fjord or the sea in the distance; oak, birch, hazel.

## Palette
Moss green, honey and straw (thatch), warm grey-brown weathered oak, pale ochre-grey daub, wet brown mud, a cool blue
morning sky with low haze, the fjord a grey-blue strip.

## Light strong enough for the puppets
The low sun (morning: cool and pale from the front left; evening: warm and low). Indoors the hearth. Nothing else.
