# You wake up with an eagle's vision

A comedy short on the user's own script. The joke rests on one true fact: birds of prey have far sharper eyesight than people (a wedge-tailed eagle resolves roughly twice the fine detail of a human eye), but sharp eyes do nothing for attention, so you can spot a bird in the sky and still walk into a glass door. The extreme zooms in the video are exaggerated for comedy. The script states no factual numbers; the sourced claim below is background for the description only.

## Claims
- The wedge-tailed eagle's visual acuity is about 140 cycles per degree, roughly double the human limit of about 60 (review of about 600 species by Caves, Brandley and Johnsen, 2018) — https://www.futurity.org/visual-acuity-vision-eyes-animals-1772002/
- That review estimated acuity from eye anatomy or behavioural tests, not an eagle eye chart; the researchers note eye measurements do not capture what the brain does with the signal — https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/558551
- A behavioural study of the wedge-tailed eagle compared its contrast sensitivity with humans at low light levels — https://newt.phys.unsw.edu.au/~jw/reprints/ReymondWolfe.pdf
