# Airliner at cruise: cabin with oxygen masks down, and the aircraft above the clouds

I looked at these in the browser. Nothing was copied or downloaded, and no airline names or liveries are used.

- Image search, cabins with the oxygen masks deployed:
  https://www.bing.com/images/search?q=airplane+cabin+oxygen+masks+deployed+passengers
  (narrowbody economy cabins, A320 and 737, after a decompression; passengers wearing the masks)
- Image search, a white airliner cruising over a cloud deck:
  https://www.bing.com/images/search?q=airliner+cruising+above+clouds+side+view+photo

## What makes the cabin read as itself
- **Masks hang in clusters** of 3-4 per row from an open drop door in the passenger service unit under the
  overhead bins. Each mask is a yellow cup on a long clear tube with a clear, limp bag, and the tubes tangle.
  The drop doors hang open on their hinges.
- **Overhead bins** are curved white or cream doors that run the full length of the cabin. Under them is the
  service strip with reading lights and air nozzles. A cove light runs along the ceiling.
- **Seats:** dark navy fabric backs with a white or grey headrest cover. Three abreast on each side of a
  narrow aisle, each with a grey tray table on its back.
- **Windows:** small rounded rectangles in a cream sidewall, with the shades half up. Bright daylight comes through.
- **Narrow aisle** with a grey-blue carpet. The cabin narrows toward the far end, where a bulkhead sits.

## What makes the exterior read as itself
- A white tube fuselage, slightly grey on the belly, with a row of small dark windows and a black cockpit windscreen band.
- Low swept wings with upturned winglets and two engines in nacelles under the wings. The tail has a swept fin
  and swept stabilisers.
- It cruises over a flat sea of cumulus. The sky is deep blue overhead and pales at the horizon. A low sun
  catches the top of the fuselage.

## Light for puppets
- **Cabin:** daylight through the windows is the strong source. It is directional from the window side, a pale
  warm-white. The ceiling cove light is soft and does not light the puppets.
- **Exterior:** no puppets.

## Chosen for a 9:16 frame
1. Masks hanging in the foreground and midground, yellow cups dangling at head height.
2. Seat backs with headrest covers lining the aisle, each with a shocked passenger.
3. A row of windows on one side, bright daylight with the cloud deck visible through them.
