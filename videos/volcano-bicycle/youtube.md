## Title
A Volcano May Have Put a German on the First Bicycle 🌋 #Shorts

## Description
A volcano on the other side of the world may have put a German on the first bicycle.

In April 1815 Mount Tambora in Indonesia erupted, the most powerful eruption in recorded history. Its sulphur spread through the sky and the climate cooled, and in Europe 1816 became the Year Without a Summer: cold rain, failed harvests, rising food prices. Oats grew scarce and costly, and many horses starved or were slaughtered for meat and hides. In Mannheim, Karl Drais built a wooden running machine: two wheels in a line, a seat and a handlebar to steer, and no pedals, so you pushed off the ground with your feet and glided. On June 12, 1817, he rode it from Mannheim to a coaching inn and back in about an hour. His biographer, historian Hans-Erhard Lessing, argues that the starving horses inspired him; it is a hypothesis, and nobody can prove it. Pedals came decades later.

Would you have tried riding a bicycle with no pedals? 👇
