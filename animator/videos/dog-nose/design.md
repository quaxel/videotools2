# Design loop — dog-nose

Every set and character is scored on the design board (composition `DogNoseDesign`, stills with the post chain and
puppet lighting on) before production. Threshold: **8/10 per asset**, at most 4 rounds.

## Rubric (0-2 each, 10 total)
1. **Reads in 1 s** at phone size in 9:16: subject clear, silhouette readable, not cluttered.
2. **Pixar look**: rounded forms, smooth soft shading, warm bounce light, depth/haze, rich dressing; 3D, not flat.
3. **Colour identity**: its dominant colour differs from the neighbouring locations; pleasing palette.
4. **Story prop legible**: the gag object (socks, pizza, feed cards, chicken, bins, the judging stare) reads instantly.
5. **Holds up at weird angles**: the low / top-down / macro / Dutch view looks intentional, no holes, no floating parts.

## Rounds

Trees blocked the chase/top-down/corner cameras, the puppet fell out of frame, night was near black, feed cards hidden, street sign mirrored, grass blades read as spikes. Fixed before scoring.

### Round 4 — independent reviewer (fresh, no memory of round 2), average 6.9
bedroom 8, monster low 7, monster macro 8, pizza 3, kitchen 7, void 7, street 7, tree feed 8, golden chase 8,
top-down 5, corner 5, bins night 7, rocky macro 7, line-up 6, nose gag 8, headlines 9.
Fixes: one hero 3D pizza slice with cheese strings; sock stripes, heel and toe seam on the monster; light shafts moved
off the characters (they were washing out faces and the monster's arm); rounded soft bins, hydrant, mailbox;
clapboard siding on houses; fur texture and a lower collar on the dogs; sunbeams through the street tree; bigger
icons in the void; top-down replaced by a 55° high angle (the flat puppet reads as a sticker from straight above);
corner and line-up reframed with a 10 % safe margin.

### Round 5 — average 6.8; Round 6 — average 6.9
Plateau: reviewer-to-reviewer noise of about ±1 on the same asset. Fixed the defects every reviewer named: sock
heel/toe patches (read as bones), stray cheese string, porch roofs now carried on a beam and brackets, kitchen fry-up
1.5x with counter dressing, three icons only in the void, drumstick turned horizontal, high-angle chase lowered.

### Round 7 — average 7.5 (10 of 16 at 8+)
bedroom 7, monster low 5, monster macro 8, pizza 5, kitchen 8, void 7, street 8, tree feed 9, golden chase 6,
chase high 5, corner 8, Rocky night 9, Rocky macro 9, line-up 8, nose gag 9, headlines 9.
Found: the monster's "glowing arm" was the bedside lamp's bloom halo behind it and the "green blob" was you hidden
behind it; the socks' white heel/toe balls made them read as bones; the drumstick's crispy bumps read as holes.
Fixed: lamp glow down, you moved clear, sock patches in the sock's own colour, smooth glossy drumstick with a big
white bone, desk dressing behind the pizza, lighter hedges and bins, overflowing bag on the bin.

Production staging (from the production stills, not the board): the bins sat between the road-side camera and you in
every trash-can shot, so that sequence is now shot from the lawn side toward the road, with you forced front-on.

### Round 8 — average 7.0
bedroom 7, monster low 4, monster macro 7, pizza 6, kitchen 7, void 7, street 8, tree feed 9, golden chase 5,
chase high 5, corner 7, Rocky night 8, Rocky macro 7, line-up 8, nose gag 8, headlines 9.

Changed after round 8, **not scored**: socks flattened like knit fabric with a clearer heel bend; Rocky's brows
levelled and set low on the lids (read sad before); the drumstick turned so its white bone sticks out ahead of the
meat (it pointed back into the ribbon), recoloured roasted gold-brown so it separates from the gold scent, glow
reduced; the chicken and high-angle chase cameras re-aimed on it.

**Status: the threshold of 8/10 was not reached.** Scored averages 6.1, 6.9, 6.8, 6.9, 7.5, 7.0: a plateau around
7-7.5 with about ±1 point of reviewer-to-reviewer noise on the same asset. Production was written from round 4 on
in parallel, and rendered after round 8.
