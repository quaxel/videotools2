Looked at in the browser (stills from published video of the experiment and its re-creations; not copied):

- Image search for the circular-track experiment — https://www.bing.com/images/search?q=Sugiyama+circular+track+traffic+jam+experiment+22+cars
- The paper's own description (track at a driving school, 360-degree camera at the centre) — https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/93262/NewJPhys_10_033001.pdf

## What makes it read as this place
- A huge, flat, empty **grey asphalt lot**; a single painted **circle** (one lane) about 73 m across, edged by
  white line or low kerb. Nothing else on the lot: no lights, no signs, no lane merges.
- **22 ordinary small cars** (white, silver, dark blue, red) spread round it at nearly even spacing.
- A **mast with a camera** in the very centre of the ring looking out at the cars.
- Beyond the lot: a flat horizon of low buildings, fields and a few trees; big pale sky.
- From high up, the jam reads as a short **clump of cars bunched nose to tail** while the rest are spread out.

## Palette
Asphalt grey (#7d8590), painted line white, grass/field green (#6aa84f), sky pale blue (#bfe6ff), cars white/silver
with a few red/blue/yellow.

## Light for puppets
Flat overcast daylight: no strong source. Brake lights are small; the puppets stay as drawn.
