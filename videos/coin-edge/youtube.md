## Title
The Coin That Wrote a Warning on Its Edge 🪙 #Shorts

## Description
He clipped silver off every coin, until the coin itself told on him.

In 1690s London, silver coins were hammered by hand and their edges were ragged, so clippers snipped slivers off and spent the coins at full value. Machine-struck coins carried a sentence round the rim: DECUS ET TUTAMEN, "an ornament and a safeguard". Cut one sliver and the sentence breaks. In 1696 the Royal Mint, then inside the Tower of London, called in the old coins, and the recoinage was finished within three years. Its Warden, Isaac Newton, hunted clippers and counterfeiters and interviewed criminals himself.

Have you ever looked at the edge of the coins in your pocket? 👇
