# Research: Why Razor Refills Are Locked Up Like Jewelry

## Mechanism

Cartridge razors run on the razor-and-blades model: the handle is priced low to get you in, and the money comes
from the refills you keep buying. A razor sells for about $10 while an eight-pack of refills runs close to $40.
A materials breakdown of a five-blade refill puts the steel well under five cents, the plastic housing at three to
five cents and coatings plus packaging at a few cents more: under thirty cents in all, against a five-dollar shelf
price, so the shelf price is fifteen to twenty times the material value. The safety razor with a throwaway blade was
patented in 1904. Since then makers have kept adding blades to justify new, pricier refills: two blades in the
early 1970s, seven by 2017.
