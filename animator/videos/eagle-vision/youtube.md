## Title
You Wake Up With Eagle Vision... and Walk Into a Glass Door 🦅 #Shorts

## Description
You wake up with an eagle's vision and suddenly you can see EVERYTHING!

A fly on the wall, a bird a mile up in the sky, your neighbor picking his nose across the street. Then your crush waves at you, you wave back, and she was waving at the dog. Even eagle eyes can't save you from a glass door. Eagles really do see about twice as much fine detail as people; the extreme zooms here are exaggerated for comedy.

Would you take eagle eyes if it came with a pigeon brain? 👇

Sources:
• Futurity on the 2018 Caves, Brandley and Johnsen acuity review (wedge-tailed eagle about 140 cycles per degree, humans about 60): https://www.futurity.org/visual-acuity-vision-eyes-animals-1772002/
