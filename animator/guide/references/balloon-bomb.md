## The balloon

- A round envelope about 10 m across of laminated washi paper: pale grey-white, the paper panels (gores) visible
  as seams running pole to pole, darker patches where the paste overlaps.
- Shroud lines start on a load band round the lower half and converge to a point well below the envelope.
- The carriage: a four-spoked cast-aluminium ring with ~32 small sandbags hanging round its rim, an aneroid control
  box in the middle and the bombs below (one 15 kg HE bomb, four small incendiaries).

## Launch (Japan)

- Sites on the Pacific coast of Honshu (Ichinomiya in Chiba, Otsu, Nakoso). Long flat beaches of dark grey sand,
  a windbreak of Japanese black pines (twisted trunks leaning seaward, flat pads of dark needles).
- Winter launches before the sunrise onshore breeze: dawn over the Pacific, the sky orange at the sea horizon,
  deep blue above. A crew of ~30 in khaki held the envelope down with guide ropes, then let go.

## The forest (Oregon, May 1945)

- Open ponderosa pine forest on the slope of Gearhart Mountain: tall straight trunks with orange-brown bark broken
  into plates by dark fissures, branches only high up, needle tufts at the branch ends.
- Floor of dry brown needles, green bunchgrass, low bitterbrush, grey rocks and fallen logs. A snowy summit behind.

## Light for puppets (strong sources only)
Launch beach: the low dawn sun on the sea horizon in front of them — a warm rim on the edges facing it.
Forest: midday sun high behind-left (no puppets in the example shot).

## Added for the full video

Above the clouds (jet stream)
- At 30,000-35,000 ft the cloud deck is far below: a flat white sea of cumulus tops with blue shadows, the sky a
  deep, nearly navy blue overhead fading to pale at the horizon. At night: a moon, stars, the cloud tops silver-blue.
- The Pacific Northwest arrival: breaks in the deck show dark green forested coast, surf lines and snowy peaks.

Snow fields (Alaska, Montana, Michigan finds, winter 1944-45)
- Alaska: flat white tundra, low black spruce spires, overcast pale sky. Montana: rolling snowy range land, barbed
  wire fence on wooden posts, Rockies behind, low bright sun. Michigan: snowy farm field, red barn, bare trees, dusk.
- A landed balloon: the paper envelope collapsed in a long crumpled heap on the snow, lines trailing to the ring.
