# Lab inside her head (telescope room) — visual references

Looked at (not copied; translated into the kit's cel style):
- Observatory telescopes and domes — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=refracting+telescope+observatory+interior&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## What makes it read as this place
- A long white or pale-blue tube on a heavy cast-iron mount (pier) with a counterweight bar, a small finder scope and an eyepiece at the back.
- Curved dome ribs overhead with a slit opening onto the night sky; a wooden step ladder by the eyepiece.
- For the lab: steel benches, gauges, a metal strongbox, a big knife switch on a panel.

## Palette
Metal blue (#3a5a8a), steel grey (#8a9ab0), white tube, brass details (#d4a63a), dark sky through the slit.

## Light for puppets
Cool screen/instrument glow from the panel: a pale-blue point light when a puppet is close; otherwise none.
