## Title
They Blamed the Big Bang on Pigeon Poop 🐦📡 #Shorts

## Description
The oldest light in the universe is all around you, and two men blamed it on pigeon poop.

New Jersey, 1964: Arno Penzias and Robert Wilson wanted to hear the Milky Way with a giant 20-foot horn antenna, but their receiver kept picking up a faint, steady hiss, day or night, in every direction, even pointed at New York City. A pair of pigeons was nesting in the horn, so they cleaned out what Penzias called a "white dielectric substance" and sent the birds away. Two days later the pigeons were back, and the hiss never changed. It was the leftover glow of the Big Bang, cooled to about three degrees above absolute zero: the cosmic microwave background. At Princeton, Robert Dicke's team was about to look for it ("We've been scooped"), and in 1978 Penzias and Wilson won the Nobel Prize in Physics.

Would you have blamed the pigeons too? 👇
