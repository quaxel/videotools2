# Sunset balcony — visual references

Looked at (not copied; translated into the kit's cel style):
- Apartment balconies — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=apartment+balcony+plants&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image
- Small balcony elements (shelf of pots, fairy lights, folding table for two, decking/tile) — https://www.houzz.com.sg/magazine/11-elements-you-need-to-style-your-small-balcony-stsetivw-vs~79868299
- Pink/violet dusk skylines — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=pink+sunset+city+rooftops&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## What makes it read as this place
- Narrow concrete slab with a metal railing (thin vertical balusters, flat handrail) or a glass panel; railing planter boxes hooked over the top rail.
- Pots at mixed heights on a small stepped shelf: trailing ivy, herbs, a geranium, one tall leafy plant in a corner.
- Warm string lights zig-zagging overhead about head height, clipped along the railing or wall.
- One folding bistro chair (and a tiny round table with a mug), a small outdoor rug on wood decking or tile.
- Beyond the rail: rooftops at different heights, chimneys, water tanks, antennas, a few lit windows, in flat layers for parallax.
- Sun low and large, half behind far rooftops; sky graded pink at the horizon into magenta then violet overhead, a few lit cloud streaks.

## Palette
Sky low pink (#ff9bb3), magenta (#d94a8c), violet (#6c3d9a), deep indigo top (#2e2257), sun (#ffd9a0), rooftop silhouettes (#3b2a4f), string lights (#ffe1a8), leaf green (#4f7a52). Keep orange to a thin rim on the sun only.

## Light for puppets
The low sun is the strong source: a pink-warm back/side rim from behind the rooftops. String lights are weak warm fill; the violet sky is ambient only.
