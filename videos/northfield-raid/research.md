# Research — northfield-raid

On 7 September 1876 eight men of the James–Younger gang rode into Northfield, Minnesota, to rob the First National Bank. Inside, three of them ordered the staff to open the safe. Acting cashier Joseph Lee Heywood said it was on a chronometer, a time lock, and could not be opened. A time lock is a clockwork in the safe door that keeps the bolts from moving until the hour it is set to; but Heywood's chronometer had not been set and the safe itself was unlocked. The robbers believed him and never tried the handle. When one of them stepped into the vault, Heywood threw himself against the vault door to shut him in; the outlaw got out with a bruised arm. Teller Alonzo Bunker ran out the back door and was shot in the shoulder. Outside, townspeople had noticed the lookouts and armed themselves; a hardware store owner (A. R. Manning) and a medical student (Henry Wheeler) shot from the street and from a window. The gang fled with $26.70 while the cash in the vault lay untouched. Heywood was shot dead for refusing. Three gang members died and three were imprisoned; the gang never recovered.

## Left out
- The cash in the vault: OCC says $15,000, Wikipedia "over $12,000". Sources disagree, so the script says only that the money stayed.
- "Seven minutes": only one source (djjd.org) gives it.
- Whether Jesse or Frank James was the man inside; accounts disagree on who shot Heywood. No robber is named on screen.
- Whether a second bandit caught the vault door: accounts disagree; we only show Heywood throwing the door and the outlaw getting out.
- Storekeepers handing out guns: only magazine accounts (True West), so the script says the merchant grabs his own rifle.
- Rock-throwing citizens, Nicholas Gustafson: not in the sources opened.
