# Mountain with an endless ladder (via ferrata) — visual references

Looked at — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=via+ferrata&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image
(steel rungs bolted into limestone walls, a cable along the route, a plank bridge between pinnacles).

## What makes it read as this place
- Pale grey limestone walls in vertical slabs and cracks, ledges with tufts of grass and small pines.
- Steel ladder rungs bolted straight into rock, a steel cable running beside them.
- A deep green valley far below and ranges of mountains fading into haze behind, snow on the highest.
- Clouds below the top of the climb, so the route disappears into them.

## Palette
Stone grey (#9a9aa6), shadow (#5d5d70), snow (#f2f4ff), pine (#2f5a4a); sky re-lit cold blue or dusk violet.

## Light for puppets
Low sun as a distant light on the climbing shots only (warm side light, soft rim).
