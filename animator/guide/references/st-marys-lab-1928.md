# St Mary's Hospital lab, 1928 (Alexander Fleming's lab) — reference notes

The sets are a video's own props, not the shared kit.

Sources (read, not downloaded): the Alexander Fleming Laboratory Museum write-ups —
https://www.ianvisits.co.uk/articles/the-alexander-fleming-museum-has-reopened-56530/ ,
https://diamondgeezer.blogspot.com/2026/01/the-alexander-fleming-laboratory-museum.html ,
https://britainexpress.com/London/Alexander_Fleming_Laboratory_Museum.htm ; Wikipedia "Discovery of penicillin"
(photo of the lab window over Praed Street).

Details that make it read:
- A small second-floor room; a long wooden bench right in front of a tall sash window looking onto Praed Street
  (brick fronts and shopfronts opposite). The window was kept shut (do not show it open).
- On the bench: stacks of glass Petri dishes, brown reagent bottles, stoppered test tubes in a rack, a brass
  microscope, a Bunsen burner; papers and notes. Crowded, nothing put away.
- Shelves of jars and bottles on the side wall; plain painted plaster walls (cream/green), dark wood.
- A warm incubator: a metal cabinet with a glass inner door, shelves of dishes, a thermometer on top.
- Palette: warm wood browns, cream walls, brown glass, brass; soft daylight from the window.
- Strong light for puppets: the window (daylight from the back), a bench lamp; the incubator's warm glow at night.
