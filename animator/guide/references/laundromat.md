# Laundromat — visual references

Looked at (not copied; translated into the kit's cel style):
- Laundromat interiors — https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=laundromat+interior&title=Special:MediaSearch&type=image

## What makes it read as this place
- A long row of front-loading washers with round glass portholes in a steel front, a coin/control panel strip above each door.
- A second row of dryers stacked two high on the back wall, with bigger round doors.
- A free-standing folding table (steel top) in the middle of the floor; wire laundry carts on castors.
- Fluorescent tube panels in a white suspended ceiling: hard, slightly blue-green light.
- Chequered or plain vinyl floor tiles, white walls with a coloured stripe band, small printed notice signs above the machines.
- Plastic chairs along a wall, a vending/soap machine, a wall clock.

## Palette
White steel (#e8eef0), porthole glass (#8fd3d8), turquoise stripe (#1fb5b0), grey-green floor tiles, one orange accent (carts, chairs).

## Light for puppets
The fluorescent tubes are the strong source: a pale cool-green distant light from above-front. Porthole glow is decorative only.
